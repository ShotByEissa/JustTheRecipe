Simple Scrambled Eggs

The perfect breakfast in just 5 minutes!

Ingredients

3 large eggs
1 tablespoon butter
2 tablespoons milk
Salt and pepper to taste

Method

Step 1: Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk with milk, salt, and pepper until well combined.

Step 2: Melt butter in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat.

Step 3: Pour in the egg mixture and let it sit for 20 seconds.

Step 4: Using a spatula, gently push the eggs from the edges to the center, forming soft curds.

Step 5: Continue folding until eggs are just set but still moist. Remove from heat immediately.

Step 6: Serve right away while still creamy.
