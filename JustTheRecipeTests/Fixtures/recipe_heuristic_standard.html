Grandma's Apple Pie

This is my grandmother's famous apple pie recipe that has been passed down for generations.

Serves: 8 Total Time: 1 hour 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup cold butter, cubed
  • 6-8 tablespoons ice water
  • 6 large apples, peeled and sliced
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 2 tablespoons butter

Instructions

  1. Combine 2 1/2 cups flour and salt in a large bowl. Cut in cold butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  2. Gradually add ice water, mixing until dough forms a ball. Divide in half, wrap in plastic, and refrigerate for 1 hour.
  3. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  4. In a large bowl, combine sliced apples, sugar, 2 tablespoons flour, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
  5. Roll out one portion of dough and place in a 9-inch pie plate.
  6. Add apple filling and dot with butter.
  7. Roll out remaining dough and place over filling. Trim and crimp edges. Cut slits in top crust.
  8. Bake for 45-50 minutes until crust is golden and filling is bubbly.
  9. Cool on a wire rack before serving.

Serve warm with vanilla ice cream for the best experience!
